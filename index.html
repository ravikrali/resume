Enterprise data and AI architecture leader with 25+ years turning complex, regulated data environments into governed platforms, reusable data products, and executable transformation roadmaps — across life sciences and CRO operations, financial services, healthcare, insurance, energy, and consulting.

My work sits at the seam most organisations struggle with: deep enough in architecture to design the canonical model, the knowledge graph, the RAG pipeline and the governance controls — and senior enough to run the P&L, build the team, and get a C-suite to fund a multi-year programme.
